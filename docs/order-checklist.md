# Order checklist - PCBs at JLCPCB (for Dan)

Written 2026-11-22 by job orderprep. Plain words, one board at a time. The source for every value is the board's
`fab-notes.md` (generated by `tools/gen_fab_jlcpcb.py`); if this page and a `fab-notes.md` ever differ, the fab notes win.
PCB field names and values were checked on 2026-11-23 against JLCPCB's live quote page (https://cart.jlcpcb.com/quote ,
4-layer view) and the "Copper Weight" help article (https://jlcpcb.com/help/article/jlcpcb-copper-weight : inner 0.5 oz
default, 1 oz / 2 oz on 4-layer boards). The assembly (PCBA) fields only show after a Gerber upload, so their names are
not checked live; the page changes now and then, so a name may read a little differently. Not checked here: prices, and
JLCPCB's live stock (needs your login).

## Before you start

1. The Gerber zip files are not stored in git. Make them fresh (or ask Claude to): from the repo folder run
   `"%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\KiCad\9.0\bin\python.exe" tools/gen_fab_jlcpcb.py` . It writes one zip per board (paths below).
2. Then run `python tools/copper_order.py --check` : it must end with `RESULT PASS` (copper on this page = fab notes = board files).
3. You place **three orders** (one per board design). One PDM needs: **2 x board A, 1 x board B, 3 x input board**.
   JLCPCB sells bare boards in lots of 5: order 5 of each. For assembly pick the smallest assembled quantity the page
   offers that covers the need (2 for A, 1 for B, 3 for the input board; the page usually offers 2 or 5: not checked).

## What is different between the three orders (the short version)

| | Board A | Board B | Input board |
|---|---|---|---|
| Need for one PDM | 2 | 1 | 3 |
| Layers | 4 | 4 | 2 |
| Size (JLCPCB reads it from the zip) | 72.4 x 95.3 mm | 111.4 x 95.3 mm | 66.1 x 64.1 mm |
| Outer copper weight | 1 oz | 1 oz | 1 oz |
| **Inner copper weight** | **1 oz (change it!)** | **1 oz (change it!)** | not shown |
| Remark to paste | yes | yes (longer) | yes (short) |
| Parts through global sourcing | Y1 | Y1, T1-T8 | none |
| Press-fit terminals you fit yourself | J1, J2 | J1, J3 | none |

Why board B gets 1 oz inner copper: with the default 0.5 oz the board runs 136-137 C at the 150 A limit (limit 130 C);
with 1 oz it runs 117-118 C (Dan's choice c1 = a, 2026-11-22; numbers in `docs/order-readiness.md`).
Why board A gets 1 oz inner copper too: at the default 0.5 oz the copper screen (`tools/verify_copper.py`) reads an
11.9 K no-sink hot spot (limit 10 K), at 1 oz 10.0 K; and one inner setting for A and B is simpler (coordinator default,
2026-11-22; table in `tools/copper_order.py`).

### 3. PCB remark (paste this whole paragraph)

> Press-fit holes: footprints J1, J2 (8 holes each, Wurth 7461084 press-fit terminal). Finished hole 1.475 mm +-0.05 mm (as in the drill file), round, plated; hole wall copper 25-60 um (Wurth 7461084 data sheet). Board thickness must not be below 1.6 mm (terminal range 1.6-3.2 mm). Via in pad: 58 vias sit in SMD pads (fab-notes.md lists the parts): please epoxy-fill and cap them (plated over, flat) so no solder wicks into them. Copper: outer layers 1 oz, inner layers 1 oz (35 um finished) as selected on the order form: please do not build this board with 0.5 oz inner copper.

### 6. In the placement preview (the picture of the parts on the board)

- **D2** (the big diode near the power terminal): its body sits centred between its one wide pad and its lead pad, not shifted to one side.
- LEDs D5, D16, D17 and the small diodes: the marked end (cathode bar / green mark) is on the pad the board's silkscreen marks.
- Every chip (U2 to U13 and the power switches): the dot / notch for **pin 1** is at the pin-1 mark on the board. The files already turn these parts the way JLCPCB expects (checked part by part by `tools/verify_cpl.py`), so they should look right; if one looks turned, do not fix it by hand: stop and tell Claude.
- J5 (USB-C) and J6 (the white 12-pin connector) sit on their pads with the opening facing the board edge / upward as drawn.
- Y1 (crystal, 2 pads) has no direction: any way round is fine.

### 7. What JLCPCB must confirm before they build

- The **press-fit holes**: finished 1.475 mm +-0.05 mm, and how thick the copper on the hole wall is. The terminal maker (Wurth) wants 25-60 um; JLCPCB publishes an 18 um average. If they answer "under 25 um", do not approve: tell Claude.
- Board thickness not below 1.6 mm.
- Epoxy-filled and capped vias as asked.

### 8. What arrives NOT fitted, and how to fit it

- Two press-fit power terminals per board (Wurth 7461084, positions J1, J2): buy 4 for the two boards (LCSC C6828431 / Digi-Key 732-3203-ND). They are pressed in, never soldered:
  arbor press, one straight stroke, the board lying on a plate with a 1.75 mm hole under every pin (8 pins), at least 40 N per pin; keep 3 mm away from other parts.
- Leave the CAN_TERM jumper (JP1) open.
